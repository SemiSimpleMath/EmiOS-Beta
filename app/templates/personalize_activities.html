An LLM agent (dayflow_cron_tickets) sits between your thresholds and the user. Its policy today:

  • Default to no-op. "Doing nothing is the correct answer most of the time."
  • Veto: never suggest an activity already in active tickets; never within 2h of a recently dismissed instance of the same type.
  • Threshold integrity: normally won't fire before the threshold is met.
  • Override (fire earlier): only when ALL true — high stakes, context evidence (just finished a walk, fatigue/pain mention, narrowing window), time-sensitive, and infrequent.
  • Stale window: skip if >60 min past the scheduled start ("don't remind about the 9 PM walk at 10:45 PM").
  • One per minute: at most one suggestion per pulse.
